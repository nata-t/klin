A new klin mark: an egg with its first crack, the cap lifted. It isn't a letter, so next to the name it doesn't read "K klin". The terminal keeps the KLIN wordmark (in the full-screen header and the banner); `--help` puts the small egg beside the name. New projects get the egg as their placeholder favicon, in `<Logo>` (on your brand color), in the landing page's terminal and in the email header.
