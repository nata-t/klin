A new klin logo: an egg with its first crack, the cap lifted. It isn't a letter, so next to the name it no longer reads "K klin". The CLI draws it small, as pixel art, beside the name in the banner and `--help`; in the banner the egg hatches — it wobbles, cracks and the cap pops up. New projects get it as their placeholder favicon, in `<Logo>` (on your brand color), in the landing page's terminal and in the email header.
