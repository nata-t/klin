A new klin logo: an egg with its first crack, the cap lifted. It isn't a letter, so next to the name it no longer reads "K klin". The CLI's full-screen header, the banner and `--help` draw it as pixel art (sized to fit the terminal); the first time the screen opens, and in the banner, the egg hatches — it wobbles, cracks and the cap pops up. New projects get it as their placeholder favicon, in `<Logo>` (on your brand color), in the landing page's terminal and in the email header.
