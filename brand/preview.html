klin brand

The mark is an egg with its first crack, the cap lifted: something new hatching, out of a clean shell. It's one colour, always gray: ink on light, white on dark. Next to it, a geometric lowercase klin. The mark isn't a letter, so the name is only said once.

Lockup

In the terminal

The CLI draws the same mark as pixel art, two square pixels per character (▀ ▄ █), in a chrome gradient — from packages/create-klin/src/ui/logo-art.ts, which the website hero and the favicons read too. Its screens use the small egg, in the corner beside the version; the first time the screen opens, the egg hatches: it wobbles, cracks and the cap pops up.

Using it

Clear space

Keep the cap's height free on every side.

Smallest sizes

  • Mark: 16 px tall (below 48 px use the favicon files — the pixel cut).
  • Lockup: 80 px wide on screen, 20 mm in print.

Don't

  • Color it, or add gradients, shadows or outlines.
  • Rotate, mirror or stretch it, or close the crack (animations aside).
  • Set the name in another typeface next to the mark.
