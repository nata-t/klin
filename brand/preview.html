klin brand

The mark is a K drawn as a dependency graph: five package nodes on a grid, joined by wires at 0° and 45° — the shape of the monorepo klin builds. It's one colour, always gray: ink on light, white on dark. Next to it, a geometric lowercase klin whose k shares the mark's 45° chevron.

In the terminal

The CLI draws the same mark as pixel art, two square pixels per character (▀ ▄ █): nodes and letters bright, wires gray. It picks the largest that fits — from packages/create-klin/src/ui/logo-art.ts, which the website hero and the favicons read too.

Using it

Clear space

Keep one node's width free on every side.

Smallest sizes

  • Mark: 16 px tall (below 48 px use the favicon files — the pixel cut).
  • Lockup: 80 px wide on screen, 20 mm in print.

Don't

  • Color it, or add gradients, shadows or outlines.
  • Rotate, mirror or stretch it, or move the nodes.
  • Set the name in another typeface next to the mark.
